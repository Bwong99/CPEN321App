# Individual Reflections

Answer the following questions with respect to the your project since the last checkpoint. You must add at least one discussion point to each section, although you may add as many more as you want. You will submit your reflections individually, then use them as talking points during your team's retro meeting in lab.

1. **What did I accomplish this week?** Outline what you did, and roughly how many hours you spent on the project this week (it is okay if some weeks you do not spend much time because of other projects, midterms, etc.). How does this compare to what you planned to do during the last sprint planning session?

2. **What went well?** Describe any successes you or your team had this week, big or small. E.g., finishing a tricky feature, learning something new, collaborating especially effectively, reaching a specific milestone, etc.

3. **What coud have gone better?** You can discuss things you could have done better, or issues you noticed in the overall team. Be specific. All feedback should be constructive. Always assume the best of your team members. E.g., issue took significantly longer than expected, miscommunication, scope creep, unexpected blockers, etc.

4. **What can I and/or the team do differently**? Describe ideas you have to improve your work processes moving forward (can relate to points from previous questions). E.g., change meeting cadence, ask for help earlier, be more conservative when assigning tasks, break things down more, etc.

5. **How did I and/or the team use AI this sprint?** Describe what you used it for (if anything), what went well, and what did not go so well. Did you use any specific strategies? Were there any surprising outcomes? Did you learn anything that will inform your AI use going forward?
