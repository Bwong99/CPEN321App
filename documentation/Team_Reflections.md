# Team Reflections

Discuss each of the following questions as a team, using your individual reflections as talking points. Take notes in this document based on your dicussion.

1. **What went well?** Describe any successes your team had this week, big or small. E.g., finishing a tricky feature, learning something new, collaborating especially effectively, reaching a specific milestone, etc.

2. **What coud have gone better?** Be specific. All feedback should be constructive. Always assume the best of your team members. E.g., issue took significantly longer than expected, miscommunication, scope creep, unexpected blockers, etc.

3. **What can the team do differently**? Describe ideas you have to improve your work processes moving forward (can relate to points from previous questions). E.g., change meeting cadence, ask for help earlier, be more conservative when assigning tasks, break things down more, etc.

4. **How did the team use AI this sprint?** Describe what you used it for (if anything), what went well, and what did not go so well. Did you use any specific strategies? Were there any surprising outcomes? Did you learn anything that will inform your AI use going forward?

5. **Action Items** Based on the discussion above, what will your team commit to doing going forward? E.g., Alice will create a PR template on GitHub, all team members will respond to messages within 24hrs, etc.
